### 15.2 DCF Calculation

```
DCF Value:
PV of 5-year Owner Earnings = 130B × (1.08 + 1.166 + 1.260 + 1.360 + 1.469) / (1.11)^0.5
                            ≈ 130B × 6.34 × 0.951
                            ≈ 783.8B

Terminal Value:
TV = (130B × 1.469 × 1.03) / (0.11 - 0.03) = 196.7B / 0.08 = 2,459B
PV of TV = 2,459B / (1.11)^5 = 2,459B / 1.685 = 1,459B

Total DCF = 783.8B + 1,459B = 2,243B RMB
Per share = 2,243B / 19,300M shares (US-listed + HK) = RMB 116 / share
In HKD = 116 × 1.09 = **HK$127 per share**
```
